# Credits

## EVE (ME menu orb)

`eve-mesh.js` holds EVE, the figure inside the ME menu orb (`menu-orbs.js`),
taken from id-composer's `models/ethereal/posed.glb` and repacked (geometry
only). This work is based on
["Low Poly Female Base Mesh"](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-female-base-mesh-583d7e9c0de945beb30004eabac92a26)
by [Mesh-Base](https://sketchfab.com/mesh-base), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The face in the ME panel's corridor (`me-corridor.js`, chapter 2) is EVE's
head and shoulders, cropped from the same mesh, under the same licence.

## Ghost Fibers (ME corridor)

The neuron, DNA and face texture in `me-corridor.js` follows
["Ghost Fibers"](https://reactbits.dev/backgrounds/ghost-fibers) from
React Bits by David Haz (MIT + Commons Clause): its fine fibre lines and
soft glow are reworked onto 3D ribbons.
